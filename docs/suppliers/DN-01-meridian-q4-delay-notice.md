# Supplier Delay Notice — DN-01

**Supplier:** Meridian Power
**Re:** Open POs with Q4 ship dates
**Severity:** High

## Notice
Meridian notifies all customers with paralleling-PDU line items on open purchase orders that the upstream component shortage affecting PDU production has not cleared. Current PDU lead times are extended by **3–5 weeks** beyond standard. Orders with November–December ship windows are additionally exposed to holiday freight congestion.

## Affected regions
All; **US-East (Ashburn)** POs are called out specifically due to concurrent year-end permitting/inspection backlogs in the metro.

## Recommended customer action
Rebaseline any schedule that assumed standard PDU lead times. Do not commit sub-90-day delivery for Q4 orders that include PDU lineups.
