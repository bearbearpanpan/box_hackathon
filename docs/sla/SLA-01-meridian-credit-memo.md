# SLA Credit Memo — SLA-01

**Supplier / counterparty:** Meridian Power
**Project reference:** Ashburn Rack Expansion — Phase 3 (see CR-02)
**Region:** US-East (Ashburn)

## Summary
Because the Phase 3 delivery slipped from a promised 60 days to an actual 95 days, the customer invoked the service-level credit clause. A delivery credit was issued for the late milestone.

## Financial impact
- Milestone penalty credit applied (late-delivery clause triggered at >30 days slip).
- Internal margin impact flagged for the deal desk's attention.

## Relevance
Documents the real cost of over-promising on a Meridian Q4 Ashburn deal: the 60-day promise didn't just slip, it triggered a contractual credit. This is the "what it costs when sales and ops disagree" exhibit.
