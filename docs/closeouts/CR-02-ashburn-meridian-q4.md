# Project Closeout Report — CR-02

**Project:** Ashburn Rack Expansion — Phase 3
**Customer:** (internal reference) Northstar Colo
**Location:** US-East (Ashburn, VA)
**Primary supplier:** Meridian Power
**Deal size:** 1,000 kW
**Signed:** Q4 (November)

## Timeline
- **Promised lead time at signing:** 60 days
- **Actual delivery:** 95 days
- **Slip:** +35 days

## What happened
Signed in Q4 against an aggressive 60-day promise. Two compounding issues: (1) a PDU shortage — the same upstream backorder that hit Phase 2 — and (2) holiday freight congestion between Thanksgiving and New Year that delayed two switchgear shipments by over a week each. The deal desk had promised 60 days to win a competitive bid; ops never signed off on that number.

## Delay reason (primary)
PDU shortage + Q4 holiday freight congestion

## Notes for future deals
This is the cautionary tale. A 60-day Q4 Meridian Ashburn promise slipped to 95 days — a 58% overrun. Q4 freight + Meridian PDU lead time is a known-bad combination. Do not promise sub-90 days for a Q4 Meridian Ashburn deal.
