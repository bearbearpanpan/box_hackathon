# Project Closeout Report — CR-01

**Project:** Ashburn Rack Expansion — Phase 2
**Customer:** (internal reference) Northstar Colo
**Location:** US-East (Ashburn, VA)
**Primary supplier:** Meridian Power
**Deal size:** 1,100 kW
**Signed:** Q2 (May)

## Timeline
- **Promised lead time at signing:** 70 days
- **Actual delivery:** 88 days
- **Slip:** +18 days

## What happened
Meridian delivered switchgear roughly on schedule, but the paralleling PDUs arrived two weeks late due to a component backorder at Meridian's upstream vendor. Commissioning could not begin until the second PDU lineup was energized. No permit issues in this metro for this phase.

## Delay reason (primary)
PDU shortage (upstream component backorder)

## Notes for future deals
Meridian's PDU lead times run longer than their switchgear. Even in a non-peak quarter the deal slipped ~26%. Build PDU buffer into any Meridian Ashburn commitment.
