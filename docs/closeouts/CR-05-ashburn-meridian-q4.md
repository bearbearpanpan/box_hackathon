# Project Closeout Report — CR-05

**Project:** Ashburn Rack Expansion — Phase 4
**Customer:** (internal reference) Vantage East
**Location:** US-East (Ashburn, VA)
**Primary supplier:** Meridian Power
**Deal size:** 1,400 kW
**Signed:** Q4 (December)

## Timeline
- **Promised lead time at signing:** 75 days
- **Actual delivery:** 110 days
- **Slip:** +35 days

## What happened
Larger phase, signed in December. The now-familiar Q4 Meridian pattern repeated and compounded: PDU lineup backordered again, plus a county electrical permit in Loudoun that took three weeks longer than the historical norm because of a year-end inspector backlog. Freight over the holidays added the rest.

## Delay reason (primary)
PDU shortage + Q4 permit/inspection backlog

## Notes for future deals
Third Q4 Meridian Ashburn data point, third hard slip. The permit backlog is specific to year-end in this metro. Between PDU lead time, Q4 freight, and year-end permitting, Q4 Meridian Ashburn deals have never delivered under ~88 days in our history.
