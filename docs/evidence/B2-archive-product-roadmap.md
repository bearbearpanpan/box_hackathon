# Archive Product Roadmap
Document ID: B2 | Owner: Archive Storage Product Management (Daniel Okafor) | Last updated: October 8, 2026 | Status: Internal planning document
Fictional demo data. Not actual AWS product plans or commitments.
## How to read this roadmap
Committed means an approved release with a confirmed date that may be shared with customers. Target means a planning date that may move and must not be promised. Exploratory means no approved plan or date; not available for customer launch.
## Feature status
- Customer-managed key rotation reports: Committed. Release date November 15, 2026.
- Archive tier restore notifications: Committed. Release date December 1, 2026.
- Cross-Region archive replication: Target Q2 2027. Dependency: replication control plane rebuild.
- Smart Archive Search (full-text search across archived objects): Exploratory. No approved plan, no release date. Not available for customer launch. Depends on a search indexing service that has not been funded.
## Guidance for field teams
Only Committed items may appear in customer agreements, and only with their published date. Target and Exploratory items must not be included as contract commitments.
