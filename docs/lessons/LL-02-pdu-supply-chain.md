# Lessons Learned — LL-02: PDU Supply Chain

## Theme
Paralleling-PDU lead times are the single most common cause of rack-deal slips in our portfolio.

## Observations
- PDU lineups consistently lag switchgear availability.
- **Meridian Power** PDUs in particular have slipped on multiple Ashburn projects (see CR-01, CR-02, CR-05).
- Upstream component shortages recur and are not reliably forecast at quote time.

## Recommendation
Treat the supplier's PDU lead-time quote as a floor, not an estimate. For Meridian, add explicit PDU buffer. The PDU line item — not switchgear — should drive the committed delivery date.
