# Lessons Learned — LL-01: Q4 Freight & Permitting

## Theme
Deals signed in Q4 (especially Nov–Dec ship windows) carry structural schedule risk that is invisible in the ERP.

## Observations across projects
- Holiday freight congestion adds 1–2 weeks to multi-shipment power deliveries.
- Year-end permit/inspection backlogs in US-East (Ashburn) have repeatedly added ~3 weeks.
- These effects **compound** with supplier lead-time issues rather than overlapping.

## Recommendation
Apply a Q4 schedule buffer to any deal with a Nov–Dec delivery window. For Ashburn specifically, add permitting buffer. Do not let the deal desk promise standard lead times on Q4 signings.
